## Hi there! I'm Yifan 👋

I'm studying computer science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, excited about machine learning, computer vision, robotics, and building full-stack applications around those ideas. I love learning new skills, working on challenging problems, and building something cool along the way!

### What I'm Working On
- **[RoboTouch Lab](https://robotouchlab.web.illinois.edu/people/)**: Undergraduate research involving robot learning, vision-language-action models and world action models.
- **[Framelight](https://framelight.ai)**: Photography app that helps people compose shots with real-time guidance, available on the App Store.

I’ve also worked with Professor Kevin Chang in the FORWARD Data Lab on retrieval-augmented generation for long-document question answering, building tools to trace failures from retrieved evidence and exploring ways to improve evidence aggregation.

I'm always happy to talk about robot learning, computer vision, or a project we could build together.

You can reach me at **yifan65 [at] illinois [dot] edu** or my [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/luo-yifan).
